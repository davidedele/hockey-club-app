# What the club has in SportEasy today

Read on 28 October 2026 by looking at the pages, signed in as Davide, without changing or creating anything. No personal data of members is copied here.

## Structure

1. Three SportEasy spaces: the club "Hockey Club Sgularat Milano", the teams "A Team" and "A/B", all under the account "Sgularat THM". Davide is Giocatore in all three. A separate club "Sgularat THM" shows him only as Membro, so team and club settings there can be changed only by club admins.
2. The club space shows 69 players, 6 seasons, 80 games and 261 goals in its dashboard. The members list shows 72 members. Contact address of the club on the profile is a Proton mailbox.
3. Menu of each space: Calendario, Campionati, Membri, Presenza, Statistiche, Messaggi, Pagamenti, Impostazioni.

## Members

1. Fields shown: name, email, phone (only for some), role, Genitore 1 (parent). Roles seen: Giocatore, Allenatore, Amico (friend, not a player).
2. The members page has no export. Its action menu only has "Assegnare gli allenatori a una squadra" and "Gestire i membri archiviati". There is an invite page.
3. Conclusion: no jersey number or position is shown in the list. The member list has to be copied by hand or asked from the board, as the president already said.

## Events

1. Event types seen: Allenamento, Partita amichevole, Libertas A (a league game with its championship name). Titles are free text, for example "Allenamento del Giovedì" or "Allenamento A/B della Domenica".
2. Fields on an event: type, title, date, start and end time, venue name, season, category. Example: Thursday training 22:30 to 23:55 at Palasesto, so event start to end is the booked ice, as Davide said. There is no rink field today, so the two rinks are not recorded anywhere.
3. Event tabs: Presenza, Compiti, Resoconto, Forum.
4. Attendance per person on an event: Presenza, Puntuale, Ritardo, Giustificato, Non giustificato, Infortunato, Non convocato. This matches the statuses already built in the app.
5. Per event settings: Voto dei giocatori, Migliore in campo, Voto della partita, each with who may vote and who may see.
6. The calendar page lets the viewer subscribe: Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or a link to paste into another calendar. This is the feature we planned as a calendar feed.

## Attendance and statistics

1. Presenza has Sintesi, Per eventi and Assegnazione di compiti. The summary counts per person: Convocazioni, Presenze, Assenze, Altre.
2. Statistiche has Classifica, team statistics and player statistics: games played, wins, draws, losses, goals for and against, average scores, best and worst streaks. A Team has only one game recorded this season so far.
3. Pagamenti (collections) is unused. Messaggi is used for team announcements.

## What this means for the app

1. Nothing in SportEasy blocks us from covering what the club uses: calendar, attendance with the same statuses, results, stats, messages.
2. The calendar feed is expected, since SportEasy offers it.
3. Things SportEasy has that we do not plan: payments, tasks per event (Compiti), per event forum.
4. Open: whether the club uses Messaggi for anything beyond announcements, and whether the Compiti tab is used.

## Second pass (28 October 2026, still only reading)

1. Event types offered when creating: Allenamento, Partita tra noi, Partita amichevole, Partita di campionato, Partita di coppa, Torneo, Altro evento. The form was only opened to read it and closed without saving.
2. Event form fields: name (up to 64 characters), description, single or recurring, start date and time, end date and time, meetup time, meeting point text, venue (a saved venue or another place), advanced settings. Opening an event sends a notification to members as soon as it is created.
3. Member profile fields: surname, name, nickname, email, phone, birth date, plus a page for periods of unavailability, which matches the absence periods already built. The profile shows an availability badge. No jersey number or position is shown on these pages.
4. Championships are entered by hand: a championship has a name, a format (6 against 6), a registered venue, participating teams, standings and bonus or penalty points. Matches are added by hand per round (Giornata). Example: the A Team championship "Libertas A" has one match so far. Nothing is read from the league website, so the league sync we built replaces manual entry.
5. Club trainings are recorded as separate events per group, for example "Allenamento A/B della Domenica" and "Allenamento C della Domenica", at times such as Tuesday 21:15, Thursday 22:30, Sunday 18:00 and 21:00.

## Third pass (28 October 2026, still only reading)

1. Event pages have four tabs: Presenza, Compiti, Resoconto, Forum. On a training event, Compiti (tasks to assign), Resoconto (a free text report with a publish button) and Forum are all empty, so these tabs are not used for trainings.
2. The venue of an event is a saved place with an address and a map link, for example Palasesto in Sesto San Giovanni.
3. Messaggi lists the event forums and a few group discussions. An event forum is archived after 15 days without new messages. What is written there is light: a late arrival notice, a joke. Nothing the club depends on.
4. Notifications seen in the bell: "Sei disponibile per l'allenamento del Giovedì 29 Ottobre?" and a reminder "Promemoria: sei disponibile per..." about four days before the event. The notification settings let each person choose email, phone or both, for important and minor notifications, report notifications and discussion notifications.
5. Player statistics columns: games played, wins, draws, losses, goals, assists, points, MVP votes, average rating, best rating, lowest rating, penalty minutes. It can be filtered by role. This matches what the app already shows.
6. The SportEasy pages were slow to load after a number of requests in a row. Only the pages listed above were opened and nothing was changed.

3. Who is invited to an event is chosen in the event settings: by criteria (roles Giocatore, Giocatore e allenatore, Allenatore, Giocatore occasionale, Amico, plus custom data fields) or by manual selection. A first message "Sei disponibile per ...?" goes out about 2 to 3 days before a training, and a reminder "Promemoria: sei disponibile per ...?" about 1 day before, apparently only to people who have not answered.
