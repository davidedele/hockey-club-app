# What SportEasy does today

Observed on 21 October 2026 from a player account (read only). No personal data recorded here.

## Structure
1. Three groups for the same people: A Team, A/B, and a club wide group (about 71 to 74 members). Each has its own calendar and chat.
2. Sidebar: Calendar, Championships, Members, Attendance, Statistics, Messages, Payments.
3. Championships and Payments are empty (not used).

## Event page
1. Header: date, type, teams, score, time, venue.
2. MVP podium for the best on ice, with vote counts.
3. An overall star rating of the game (1 to 6).
4. Tabs: Summary, Attendance, Tasks, Forum (a discussion thread per event).
5. Per player table: average rating, my rating (blank for yourself), presence, goals, assists, saves, shots against, penalties, time.
6. Attendance statuses: on time, late, excused, unexcused, injured, not called up.
7. Tasks: the coach can assign tasks for an event (not used).

## Training events
1. Title, start and end time, a separate meetup time ("Appuntamento"), venue.
2. Players answer Present or Absent. There is also "waiting" for those who have not answered.
3. Section for a message from the coach.
4. The club trains on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday. About 22 trainings from early September to 21 October.

## Reminders
Automatic: "are you available?" when created, then a reminder a couple of days later.
Notification settings let each user choose email, phone or both.

## Attendance reports
1. Season summary per player (called, on time, late, excused, absent, injured, not called up).
2. Per event grid of every player by every date.

## Calendar
Synchronisation with a webcal link and one tap buttons for Apple Calendar and Google Calendar.

## Messages
Event threads appear in Messages. Read receipts shown. Archived after 15 days without new messages.

## Members
Fields: surname, name, nickname, parents, email, phone, date of birth, year of arrival, height, weight, licence number, position, jersey number.

## Account
Light, dark or system appearance. Language setting. Newsletter and partner offers toggles.

## What SportEasy does not have
Line-ups, training plans or drills, weekly training limits per player.
